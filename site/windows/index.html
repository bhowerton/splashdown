Three steps

  1. 1

    Open the file you downloaded

    It's called Splashdown-windows-x64-setup.exe. Your browser shows it when it has finished, or you'll find it in your Downloads folder. Double-click it.

  2. 2

    Click through the installer

    There's nothing to choose. Click Next or Install until it says it's finished. You don't need to be an administrator.

  3. 3

    Splashdown starts by itself

    When the installer finishes, Splashdown opens on its own. There's nothing more to click.

What happens the first time

It sets itself up to run on your computer

A short setup guide opens. It gets Splashdown ready to run on your computer, so nothing you say leaves it. That means downloading about 3 GB. Speech comes first, so you can start dictating after about half a gigabyte, and the rest carries on downloading while you work.

Where to find it afterwards

Look for the icon by the clock

Splashdown lives in the notification area at the right end of the taskbar, by the clock. Windows may tuck it away under the ^ arrow. To keep it in view, drag its icon out of there and onto the taskbar. To dictate, hold Right Ctrl, say something, and let go.

Something's not right?

A blue box says "Windows protected your PC"

This can appear for a new release, even though the installer is signed (the publisher is Point Dynamics, LLC), until enough people have downloaded it. Click More info, then Run anyway.

I can't see the icon

Click the ^ arrow by the clock. Splashdown's icon is in the little box that opens. Drag it onto the taskbar to keep it in view.

It says nothing can be heard

Open Settings, then Privacy & security, then Microphone, and make sure Let desktop apps access your microphone is on.
