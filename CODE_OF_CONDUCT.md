# Code of conduct

Splashdown follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and patient, assume good intent, critique ideas rather than people, and leave harassment, insults and personal attacks out of every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere you represent the project).

**Reporting.** If someone's behavior makes you uncomfortable, report it privately through GitHub: on the repository's **Security** tab, choose **Report a vulnerability** and start the title with "Conduct:". Only the maintainers can see these reports. They will respond, keep the reporter's identity confidential, and act according to the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
