Words & language

The languages you speak, names to spell right, saved phrases, and words to always replace.

You and your language
Languages you mixIf you switch between languages, even mid-sentence, tick them. Works best with OpenAI or OpenRouter speech; Apple's speech model hears one language at a time.
Vocabulary
Names and terms to spell correctlyOne per line or comma-separated, e.g. product names, colleagues, jargon
Bring your words from another dictation appA dictionary or snippets export, or a word list (text or CSV, with “misspelling,correction” lines for corrections). Words, corrections and snippets are added; nothing already here changes.
Snippets
Say a phrase, get saved texte.g. say “my address” to insert your full address. Works mid-sentence too; the AI never rewords the saved text. Fill-ins: {date}, {day}, {time}, {iso-date} and {clipboard}.
Replacements
Always change one word or phrase to anotherApplied to the final text, e.g. “teh” → “the”, or “gonna” → “going to”. Whole words, ignoring case; tick Regex for a pattern ($1 for groups).
Transforms
Saved instructions you run without speakingIn Notes, from the Transform menu, on the note or the selected text. Give one a shortcut and it works on the selected text in any app (with nothing selected, on what you just dictated). The text is sent to your AI provider, the same as a spoken instruction.

Permissions

What macOS needs to let Splashdown hear the key and type for you.

You're all set. Click into any text field, hold Right Option and speak.

Two quick steps. Each one updates here by itself as soon as you allow it.

1 Accessibility Lets Splashdown notice the hotkey, see which field you're in, and type your words into it. In System Settings, switch on Splashdown. (If no window appeared, use “Open System Settings”.)
2 Microphone Only used while you hold the hotkey. Microphone access was turned off. In System Settings, switch on Splashdown under Microphone.

If the hotkey still does nothing after both are allowed, a password field or Terminal's Secure Keyboard Entry may be on. Secure input blocks all hotkeys while it's active.
